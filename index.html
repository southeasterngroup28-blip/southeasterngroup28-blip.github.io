Consumer software and direct‑to‑consumer commerce, built and operated in‑house.

RONSO LLC is an independent company based in Miami, Florida. We design, build, and run our own products end to end, from the software itself to the storefronts, checkout, and fulfillment behind it.

What we do

Software

Native mobile applications for consumer audiences, designed, engineered, and operated by the company.

Commerce

Online storefronts and limited-run physical goods, sold directly to the customer with in-house fulfillment.

Events

Ticketing and entry for live, in-person events, handled inside our own products from purchase to the door.

About

RONSO operates on a simple rule: keep everything in-house. Design, engineering, sales, and customer relationships stay under one roof instead of assembling products from rented parts.

The company is independent and privately held. We build a small number of products and run them for the long term.
